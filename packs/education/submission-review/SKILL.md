# Submission review

This skill guides procedure; it grants no tools, permissions, or authority.

Review a language-learning submission supplied by the teacher or returned by authorized platform tools. Preserve the learner's intent and voice; do not turn a review into a model essay unless asked.

## Procedure

1. Identify the prompt, supplied criteria, stated level, and submission type. If the goal or criteria are missing, ask before evaluating where that information is decisive.
2. Review only content shared in the conversation or returned by authenticated tools within their documented scope. Do not search for, transmit, or complete the submission with other external data.
3. Consider task alignment, organization, intelligibility, grammar, vocabulary, collocations, and register. Prioritize patterns that affect meaning or fluency instead of listing every error.
4. Provide a complete corrected version when the teacher requests it or it is useful and editable text is available. Otherwise, correct priority patterns selectively and leave space for learner self-correction. For audio without a transcript, give feedback on observable features without producing a full rewrite. Clearly separate source material, corrections, and feedback.
5. Never assign a grade unless both an explicit grading scale and explicit criteria are provided. A request for a grade alone is insufficient: end your answer with a direct question asking the teacher for the missing scale and criteria. Explain any grade using evidence and do not invent institutional standards.
6. Structure the answer in two labeled parts, in this order: a short **Feedback for the learner** followed by the complete **Notes for the teacher**. The required format for each part lives in [review quality](references/review-quality.md); when saving the correction in the BYS portal, that file also maps each labeled part to `generalFeedback`/`internalObservation` and the corrections to `rangeComments`.
7. Write the learner part in the language and variety requested; preserve the original language when appropriate. Keep the tone respectful, specific, and free of empty praise or embarrassment.
8. Label all output **REVIEW PROPOSAL**. This conversation does not change the submission or outcome in the teacher's platform/system. Apply [action confirmation](references/action-confirmation.md), [draft review](references/draft-review.md), [operational safety](references/operational-safety.md), [language context](references/language-context.md), [learning context](references/learning-context.md), and [review quality](references/review-quality.md).

## Completion criterion

Finish when the answer separates the learner feedback from the teacher notes and the learner part follows the format in [review quality](references/review-quality.md); and the correction, feedback, and any permitted evaluation are tied to the prompt and supported by the available submission evidence, including observable audio features when no transcript is available. State what could not be evaluated.

If an authenticated BYS MCP connection is available, read `references/bys-portal.md` before answering. Also read it if the teacher mentions the BYS portal.
