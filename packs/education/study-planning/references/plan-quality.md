# Study plan quality

- A study plan is individual; do not confuse it with an assigned task or lesson.
- Choose a weekly structure or a skill-based list to fit the request; do not invent an institutional format.
- Distribute time according to stated availability and include breaks or lighter days when useful.
- Make each activity short, actionable, and connected to a communicative goal.
- Balance skills when useful; avoid variety for its own sake.
- Preserve progress evidence reported by the teacher; do not mark activities complete by assumption.
- Show total planned time calculated from the stated availability and a lower-effort alternative, making clear what is optional.
