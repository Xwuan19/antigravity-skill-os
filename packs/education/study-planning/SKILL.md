# Study planning

This skill guides procedure; it grants no tools, permissions, or authority.

Organize a sustainable individual learning proposal with clear progression and activities connected to the learner's goal. Work from information supplied by the teacher and relevant data returned by authenticated tools within their documented scope. Materials follow the language requested by the teacher.

## Procedure

1. Identify the goal, known level, time period, available minutes per day or week, priority skills, and preferences. Ask for decisive missing information; do not infer a level. Calculate planned workload only from availability the teacher supplied.
2. Confirm the request is an individual study plan (a route with recurring activities), not an assigned task or class lesson plan.
3. Distribute realistic activities within the stated availability. Balance listening, speaking, reading, and writing when useful; prioritize one skill when requested.
4. For each activity, give a goal, actionable instruction, approximate duration, and simple evidence of completion. Vary guided practice and communicative use; connect activities to the stated real-world goal.
5. Use the requested language and variety in plan labels and instructions. State the learner's known level in the plan title or overview. If the teacher did not name specific study days, say so and present the days as a suggestion.
6. Present a readable overview with a routine, the total planned time calculated from stated availability, and a way to adapt if the week becomes busy. Apply [action confirmation](references/action-confirmation.md), [draft review](references/draft-review.md), [operational safety](references/operational-safety.md), [language context](references/language-context.md), [learning context](references/learning-context.md), and [plan quality](references/plan-quality.md).
7. Label the plan **PROPOSAL**. The conversation does not create or update a plan in the teacher's platform/system. Say **SAVED** or **PUBLISHED** only when the teacher supplies explicit evidence of that state.

## Completion criterion

The proposal fits the stated time, covers the goal, and makes clear what to do and how to recognize completion. If that cannot be verified, state the uncertainty and ask for clarification.
